Glossary

ProjectThe main record for a piece of work being tracked — has a title, a code, a Principal Investigator, milestones, a team, and bookings. See Projects.
PI (Principal Investigator)The person in charge of a project. Choosing or registering a PI when you create a project automatically adds them to the project's team. See Creating a project.
MilestoneA dated goal inside a project, with a status you set directly (pending, in progress, or done) as work progresses, and optional owners and instruments attached to it. See Milestones.
BookingA scheduled session — an instrument use, a consultation, or a meeting — with a date, time, people, and (usually) a cost. Bookings are the record billing is built from. See Booking a Session.
Facility staffPeople flagged as facility staff can be assigned to a booking as billable operators, paid at their own hourly rate. Regular attendees are never billed. See The Facility Staff flag.
AttendeeAnyone assigned to a booking to record who was present — distinct from facility staff, who are the ones actually billed for their time. See Facility staff vs. attendees.
RetireWhat happens to a person or instrument instead of deleting them, once they have any history attached (a past booking, a project team, a milestone). Retiring hides them from pickers for new work but keeps every past record intact. See Retire vs. delete.
ArchiveThe project equivalent of retiring — an archived project is hidden from the active registry and the dashboard, but every milestone, booking, and cost it holds stays exactly as it was. See Archive.
CancelledWhat happens to a booking instead of deleting it, once it holds any real information. A cancelled booking frees its time slot but keeps its record — and sometimes its charge. See Cancelled vs. deleted.
Billing retainedWhether a cancelled booking's charge still stands. Cancelling before the start time drops the charge; cancelling after it started keeps the charge unless an admin waives it. See The decision table.
Cost snapshotThe totals calculated for a booking are frozen in place the moment it's saved. Later changes to rates, overhead, or tax never rewrite a booking that already exists. See Cost snapshots.
Group/LabThe lab or research group a person belongs to — a free-text field that also groups bookings for discounts and reports. See Labs & departments.
Group discountA standing discount percentage set per Lab/Group, applied automatically to that lab's new bookings. See Group discounts.
OverheadA facility-wide percentage (internal or external) added on top of a booking's discounted subtotal. See Overhead.
TaxA percentage applied on top of the discounted subtotal plus overhead — the last step in a booking's cost. See Tax.
Billed perHow an instrument charges: time multiplies its price by the booking's length in hours; any other option multiplies price by an amount you type in on the booking itself. See Cost & billed per.
Admin ModeA toggle that reveals billing-sensitive controls (discounts, waiving charges). It is not a login and has no password — anyone at this browser can turn it on. See Admin Mode.
BackupA single file holding your entire tracker — every project, person, booking, and uploaded file — that you export to protect or move your data. See Exporting a backup.
RestoreLoading a backup file back into the tracker. Restoring replaces everything currently in the tracker — there is no merge. See Restoring a backup.
PWA (installable app)The tracker can be installed from your browser's own menu so it opens in its own window and keeps working offline once loaded. See Installing the app.
Vocabulary / "+ Add New"Dropdown lists (status, role, modality, and similar fields) that grow as you use the app — picking "+ Add New" and typing a value adds it to the shared list, it never literally saves the word "Other." See "+ Add New" is really "Other".
Sample (demo) dataA small, realistic set of example people, instruments, projects, and bookings you can load to practice safely, without risking real records. See Loading sample data.
ConflictAn overlap between two bookings for the same instrument or the same facility staff member on the same day. Conflicts are blocked at save time. See Conflicts.
UtilizationHow much an instrument or staff member was actually used over a date range — booked hours, billed hours, and revenue, tracked on the Reports screen. See Reports & Utilization.

Frequently asked questions

I opened the app on another computer and my data is gone. What happened?

Your data lives only in the browser on the device where you entered it — there's no account or server copy in between. Nothing is lost, but nothing followed you either. Move it across with a backup file: see Move your data to a new computer and Your data lives here.

Why did 10 minutes of staff help cost a full hour?

Facility staff time bills with a one-hour minimum, always rounded up — so anything from a few minutes up to one hour bills as exactly one hour, and one hour five minutes bills as two. See the worked numbers in Staff hours.

Why does a cancelled booking still show a charge?

Cancelling before the booking's start time drops the charge, because the slot was never actually held. Cancelling after it started keeps the charge standing, because the time was held whether or not it ran — unless an admin explicitly waives it. See The decision table.

Why is the red button always the Cancel option on a delete confirmation?

On a dialog meant to prevent an accident, the eye-catching color is put on the safe way out, not the destructive one — so a reflexive click lands on Cancel, not Delete. See it in action in Deleting a milestone.

Why can't the app just send the email itself?

There's no server behind the tracker that could send mail on your behalf, and pre-filling a full message into a mail-app link isn't reliable — those links get cut off after a couple thousand characters. See Why it can't just send the email.

I changed the tax rate — why didn't my old bookings change?

A booking's cost is frozen ("snapshotted") the moment it's saved. Changing a rate in Settings only affects bookings created after the change. See Cost snapshots and Billing rates.

Why can't I delete this person?

Because they have history — a past booking, a project team, a milestone they own. Deleting them would silently break those records, so the app retires them instead, which hides them from new work while keeping old records intact. See Retire vs. delete.

Someone's name now shows "(Retired)" — did their old records change?

No. "(Retired)" is only added when the name is displayed; the stored record is untouched, so historical bookings and milestones read exactly as they did before. See Retire vs. delete.

Does picking "Other" in a dropdown save the word "Other"?

No — "+ Add New" is really what "Other" means here. Picking it opens a box to type your own value, which is then added to that field's shared list for next time. See "+ Add New" is really "Other".

Is Admin Mode a login, and does it keep other people out?

No. It's a convenience toggle that shows or hides billing controls, with no password behind it — anyone using this browser can switch it on. See Admin Mode.
