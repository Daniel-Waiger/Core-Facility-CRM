Chapter 8

Booking a Session

A booking records who used the facility, what they used, when, and what it cost — this chapter walks through creating one from start to finish.

What you'll be able to do

  • Read the Calendar screen itself — its month grid, its two kinds of event, and the Today's Agenda dialog.
  • Start a new booking from the calendar and fill in every field with confidence.
  • Understand the Group/Lab gate — why the people picker locks, and how to unlock it.
  • Use the three pickers (people, instruments, facility staff) and read their badges.
  • Register a brand-new person without losing your half-finished booking.
  • Answer the "Single Instrument Booking?" prompt sensibly, and turn it off (or back on) if you'd rather not see it each time.
  • Read the live cost panel, add rich-text notes, and understand booking conflicts.
  • Edit an existing booking, and understand why a retired person can still show up on it.

The Calendar screen

The Calendar is a month grid, weeks running Monday to Sunday, and it's where you'll do most of your booking work.

  • Each day's cell can hold two kinds of event: 📅 bookings (sessions on the facility's equipment) and 🎯 milestones (project deadlines — see Milestones).
  • Within a day, events with a set time sort first, earliest to latest; untimed events (including all milestones, which have no time of their own) follow after.
  • Prev and Next move the grid a month at a time; Today jumps back to the current month and opens the Today's Agenda dialog — a focused list of just today's bookings and milestones (see Dashboard & Today's Agenda).
  • Click any empty space on a day to start a new booking pre-filled with that date.
  • Click an existing event to open it — a booking opens for editing (see below), a milestone opens its own edit form.

Starting a new booking

The most direct way to book a session is from the Calendar:

  1. Go to Calendar in the sidebar.
  2. Find the day you want and click anywhere on that day's empty space (not on an existing event — clicking an existing booking opens it for editing instead, see Editing a booking).
  3. A New Booking form opens, already pre-filled with the date you clicked.

Now work through the fields, top to bottom:

  1. Title (required) — a short description of the session, e.g. "Confocal imaging — cell viability."
  2. Date — already filled in from your calendar click; change it if needed.
  3. Start time and End time — these two decide the booking's length, which feeds directly into the cost calculation (see the cost panel below) and into whether this booking conflicts with another (see Conflicts).
  4. Project — optional. Pick the project this session is for, or leave it blank for a facility-wide session with no specific project attached.
  5. Group/Lab — see the next section; this one field controls what shows up in the people picker.
  6. The three pickers: Assign People, Assign Instruments, Assign Facility Staff — see below.
  7. Notes and Next Steps / Action Items — see Rich-text notes.
  8. Save.

The Group/Lab gate — and why the people picker locks

Until you choose a Group/Lab for this booking, the Assign People dropdown is locked — it won't open, and it flashes a reminder: "Choose Group/Lab First."

🔍 Why it works this way. Once you pick a Group/Lab, the Assign People dropdown only shows people who belong to that lab — it's there to help you find the right person quickly on a facility with a long directory, rather than scrolling through everyone. But it can only filter the list once it knows which lab to filter by, so until a Group/Lab is chosen, there's nothing sensible for that dropdown to show — hence the lock.

There are two ways to satisfy the gate:

  • Pick a Project first. The moment you choose a project, the Group/Lab field auto-fills itself from that project's PI's lab — one less field to fill in by hand, and the people picker unlocks immediately.
  • Pick a Group/Lab directly, if this booking has no project (a facility-wide session, say).

⚠️ Careful. The Assign Facility Staff picker is deliberately not filtered by Group/Lab — any facility staff member can run a session for any lab, so that picker always shows everyone flagged as staff regardless of which Group/Lab is selected. Only the Assign People (attendees) picker is filtered.

Adding someone from a different lab: "Show all labs"

Right next to the Assign People picker is a Show all labs checkbox. Tick it and the dropdown lists people from every lab at once, instead of only the one selected in Group/Lab — and it works even before a Group/Lab has been chosen at all, bypassing the "Choose Group/Lab First" lock.

  1. Tick Show all labs above the Assign People dropdown.
  2. Open the dropdown — everyone in the directory now appears, regardless of lab.
  3. Add the person you need; their badge appears the same as always.
  4. Leave the checkbox ticked or untick it afterward — either way, once someone's badge is on the booking, it stays there.

🔍 Why it works this way — and why billing doesn't change. The Group/Lab field still decides which lab's standing discount applies to this booking's cost — adding a cross-lab attendee through "Show all labs" never changes that. Billing is about which lab the session belongs to, not about every individual attendee's own lab, so a visiting collaborator can be listed on the booking without quietly shifting whose discount rate gets used.

🧪 Try it. Load the sample data, open a new booking, and pick a Group/Lab. Try opening Assign People — you'll only see that lab's people. Now tick Show all labs and open it again — everyone in the directory appears, but the Group/Lab field (and the discount it drives) hasn't changed.

The three pickers and how badges work

Three fields on the booking form work the same way: a dropdown to choose from, and a row of removable badges underneath showing everyone or everything you've added so far.

  • Assign People — attendees from the selected Group/Lab (see above).
  • Assign Instruments — the equipment used in this session.
  • Assign Facility Staff — the paid staff running the session (see People & Labs for what makes someone eligible to appear here).
  1. Open a picker's dropdown and choose an entry — it appears as a badge below the dropdown.
  2. Add as many as you need; each becomes its own badge.
  3. To remove someone or something, click the small remove control on their badge.

🔍 Why it works this way. A retired person or a retired instrument never appears in a picker's dropdown — you can't newly add someone who's left. But if they were already added to this booking before they retired, their badge keeps showing, because it's a record of what actually happened at the time. See Editing a booking below for more on this.

Registering a new person mid-booking

If the person you need for this booking isn't in the directory yet, you don't need to abandon the form and start over.

  1. On the New Booking form, choose Register New Person.
  2. Fill in their basic details and save.
  3. You're returned to the same booking form, exactly as you left it, with the new person now available to pick.

💡 Tip. This quick registration only asks for the essentials. If you need to set their Facility Staff flag, hourly rate, or other details, open their full record afterward from People & Labs and finish filling it in.

The "Single Instrument Booking?" prompt

The first time you pick an instrument on a new booking, you'll be asked: "Single Instrument Booking?"

  • Answer Yes if this booking only ever uses that one instrument — the Assign Instruments dropdown then locks itself, so you can't accidentally add a second one. (Remove the instrument's badge to unlock it again.)
  • Answer No if you might add more instruments to this same booking.

The prompt also carries a "Don't ask me again" checkbox. Tick it before answering, and the app stops asking on future bookings — it simply leaves the Assign Instruments dropdown unlocked every time, as if you'd answered "No," without interrupting you to ask.

💡 Tip. If you turned the prompt off and want it back — say, a new team member finds the auto-lock behavior helpful — open Settings → Preferences and turn Ask about single-instrument bookings back on. See Settings, Admin Mode & Discounts.

⚠️ Careful. If you're not sure whether to tick "Don't ask me again," leaving the prompt on is the safer default — it costs one extra click per booking, and answering No each time leaves the dropdown open exactly as if you'd turned the prompt off.

The live cost panel

As you fill in times, instruments, and facility staff, a Cost & Time Breakdown panel on the form updates itself in real time — you see the running total before you've even saved the booking.

This chapter won't repeat the arithmetic behind it — every line item, the discount, overhead, and tax are explained with full worked numbers in How Costs Are Calculated, and the same rules are what you'll see reflected here live.

💡 Tip. Whatever total you see in this panel the moment you save is frozen into the booking as a permanent record — later changes to rates, overhead, or tax elsewhere in the app won't rewrite it. See The Big Ideas.

Rich-text notes

The Notes field (and Next Steps / Action Items) supports simple formatting, not just plain text:

  • Bold and italic text.
  • Bulleted lists.
  • Four font sizes.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+B for bold, Ctrl+I for italic.

💡 Tip. If you paste text copied from somewhere else (a document, an email), it comes in as plain text — any formatting from the original source is stripped, so it won't carry over styles you didn't intend.

Conflicts are blocked at save time

The app won't let you double-book an instrument or a facility staff member. If, when you try to save, this booking's time overlaps with another booking on the same day that uses the same instrument or the same facility staff member, the save is refused outright and an error tells you why.

🔍 Why it works this way. This is a hard rule, not a suggestion — it stops the same microscope, or the same technician, from being double-booked to two sessions at once. If you need to free up a slot that's blocking you, cancelling the booking that's in the way frees it immediately: a cancelled booking never blocks a conflict check, even if it hasn't been deleted. See Cancelling & Billing Rules for how cancelling works.

⚠️ Careful. Conflicts are only checked when you try to save — there's no warning while you're still filling in the form, so you won't know about an overlap until you hit Save.

Editing an existing booking

Click any booking on the Calendar (an event that already exists, not empty space) to open it for editing. The same form appears, pre-filled with everything already saved.

🔍 Why retired people and instruments still show as badges. When you save an edited booking, the app rebuilds its list of attendees, instruments, and staff from exactly what the form shows at that moment. If a retired person's badge were silently hidden from the form, saving the edit would quietly erase them from the booking's history — which would be wrong, since they really were there. So the form always keeps showing every badge that's already on the booking, retired or not; retirement only ever affects the dropdown of new choices, never an existing badge.

⚠️ Careful. Because saving rebuilds the lists from what's on the form, don't remove a retired person's badge unless you actually mean to erase them from this booking's record — there's no way to "just leave it alone" other than not touching that badge.

Everything else about editing works the same as creating: the Group/Lab gate, the three pickers, the live cost panel, and the conflict check at save all apply exactly as described above.

🧪 Try it. Load the sample data, retire a person who attended one of the demo bookings, then open that booking to edit it. Confirm their badge is still there, now labelled "(Retired)," and that they no longer appear in the Assign People dropdown for adding someone new.

Check yourself

You open a new booking and the Assign People dropdown won't open. What's the fastest fix?

Pick a Project (which auto-fills the Group/Lab from the project's PI) or pick a Group/Lab directly — the people picker unlocks as soon as a Group/Lab is set.

Someone from a different lab needs to be added as an attendee, but the people picker only shows the booking's current lab. What do you do?

Tick Show all labs next to Assign People — it lists everyone in the directory regardless of lab. The booking's Group/Lab field, and the discount tied to it, don't change just because you added them.

You cancel a booking that was holding the only available slot on an instrument today. Can a new booking now use that same instrument at that time?

Yes — a cancelled booking stops blocking its slot immediately, so the conflict check no longer sees it in the way.

While editing an old booking, you notice a retired instrument's badge is still there. Should you remove it to "clean up"?

No — leave it. That badge is the real record of what instrument was used on that booking. Removing it and saving would erase that history from the record.
