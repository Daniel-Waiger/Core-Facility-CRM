Chapter 16 · Appendix A

Rough Edges & Workarounds

Every quirk in the app that's worth knowing about ahead of time, in one place — what you'll notice, why it happens, and what to do about it.

What you'll be able to do

  • Recognize the app's known rough edges before they surprise you
  • Understand, in plain terms, why each one happens
  • Apply the simple workaround for each
  • Know where to look if something feels "off" and it isn't listed here

None of the items below are bugs waiting to be fixed by clicking around differently — they're deliberate trade-offs, simple app behavior, or small gaps that are easiest to just know about. Nothing here will lose your data as long as you follow the workaround.

Recently smoothed out

A handful of rough edges that used to live in this appendix have since been fixed. Their old anchors are kept below (in case something elsewhere still links to one) but each now just points you to where the improved behavior is documented.

  • The browser's Back button now works. Screens have their own addresses, and Back/Forward move between them properly. See Getting Started.
  • Adding someone from a different lab no longer takes a two-step dance. A "Show all labs" toggle next to Assign People lists everyone at once. See the Group/Lab gate.
  • The "Single Instrument Booking?" prompt can now be turned off. A "Don't ask me again" checkbox on the prompt, and a matching Settings toggle to bring it back. See the single-instrument prompt.
  • A new lab or department name now sticks right away. It's saved the moment you type it with "+ Add New," even if you go on to cancel the form. See Labs & departments.
  • A misspelled lab name is now easy to fix everywhere at once. The Rename / Merge Lab tool renames a lab across every person, its discount row, and past bookings in one step. See Rename a lab or group.
  • Reports now draws charts, not just tables. The Instrument Utilisation, Funnel, and Activity Mix cards each draw a bar chart alongside their table. See Reports & Utilization.
  • The calendar isn't limited to one month at a time anymore. A Week view and a resource Timeline view sit alongside the original month grid, and the "Today" button now jumps the grid straight back to the current day/week — Today's Agenda is its own separate "Agenda" button next to it. See Month, Week, and Timeline.
  • Booking conflicts now show up while you're still filling out the form. A live, as-you-type advisory checks the same instrument/staff overlap rule used at save time, so you see a clash before you click save, not only after. See Conflicts.

There's still no general undo button

What you'll notice: once you confirm an action — delete, clear, retire — there's no "undo" to bring it back. Confirmation dialogs are the only safety net before the action happens.

Why it happens: the app doesn't keep a history of past states to roll back to; it simply is whatever's currently in the database.

Workaround: make backups a habit, not an afterthought. Export a backup before any big change, and turn on Automatic Daily Backup (and, if you're on Chrome or Edge, the silent backup folder) so you're always a few clicks from a recent copy. One safety net is now automatic: right before a restore replaces your data, the app downloads a safety copy of what you currently have — see below and Restoring a backup.

Restoring a backup still replaces everything, with no merge

What you'll notice: restoring an older backup file wipes out anything you've entered since — there's no option to keep both sets of data.

Why it happens: a restore fully swaps the database out from under the app; there's no reliable, automatic way to merge two independent sets of projects and bookings.

Workaround: the app now downloads a safety copy of your current data automatically right before a restore replaces it (skipped only when there's nothing in the database yet) — so you're covered even if you forget. It's still worth exporting a fresh backup of your current data yourself before restoring anything else, so you know exactly where the file is and what it's called. See Restoring a backup.

The silent backup folder can lose permission after a restart

What you'll notice: silent backups stop showing up in the folder you chose, with no error to explain why.

Why it happens: Chrome and Edge can forget the permission you granted to write into that folder once the browser itself restarts — this is a browser-level privacy protection, not something the tracker controls.

Workaround: open Settings and click Re-enable under the Silent Backup Folder option to grant access again. See The silent backup folder.

"Emailing" attendees is really copy, paste, and send yourself

What you'll notice: the Email Attendees feature opens a blank email in your own mail app instead of sending anything itself, and you have to paste the recipients, subject, and body in yourself.

Why it happens: this is deliberate, not a bug — the tracker has no server of its own that could send mail on your behalf, and mail-app links have a length limit too short to reliably carry a pre-filled message. See Why it can't just send the email.

Workaround: use the three copy buttons in the dialog, then paste each one — recipients, subject, body — into the blank email the app opened for you.

You can't force-delete a person or instrument that has history

What you'll notice: the same button that deletes a person or instrument with no history instead retires one that has any — you never get a choice to override that and delete anyway.

Why it happens: this is deliberate — deleting a record with history would silently break every past booking, project, or milestone that refers to it. See Retire vs. delete.

Workaround: if you're trying to merge two duplicate records (say, the same person entered twice), retire the duplicate rather than trying to delete it — the retired one stays visible on its old records but drops out of pickers for new work.

Other small things worth knowing

  • Reports' export is still spreadsheet-only: the charts on screen (see above) don't change what you get when you export — the Reports screen's export button always produces an XLSX spreadsheet of the underlying tables, with no DOCX/PDF option and no image of the charts themselves. See Reports & Utilization.
