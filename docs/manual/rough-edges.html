Chapter 15 · Appendix A

Rough Edges & Workarounds

Every quirk in the app that's worth knowing about ahead of time, in one place — what you'll notice, why it happens, and what to do about it.

What you'll be able to do

  • Recognize the app's known rough edges before they surprise you
  • Understand, in plain terms, why each one happens
  • Apply the simple workaround for each
  • Know where to look if something feels "off" and it isn't listed here

None of the items below are bugs waiting to be fixed by clicking around differently — they're deliberate trade-offs, simple app behavior, or small gaps that are easiest to just know about. Nothing here will lose your data as long as you follow the workaround.

1. The browser's Back button doesn't work the way you expect

What you'll notice: pressing your browser's Back button doesn't take you to the previous screen inside the tracker — it can leave the app entirely.

Why it happens: the tracker is a single page that changes what it shows without changing your browser's address, so the browser has no "previous page" of the app to go back to.

Workaround: always navigate using the sidebar links, not your browser's Back or Forward buttons.

2. Adding someone from a different lab to a booking is a two-step dance

What you'll notice: the Assign People picker on a booking only shows people from the Lab/Group you've selected for that booking — someone from another lab doesn't appear, even though you know they're in the system.

Why it happens: the people picker is deliberately scoped to one lab at a time, to keep long people lists manageable and to match discounts and reporting to the right group. See the Group/Lab gate.

Workaround: temporarily switch the booking's Group/Lab to the person's own lab, add them, then switch the Group/Lab back — their badge stays on the booking either way. The Assign Facility Staff picker is never filtered by lab, so this only ever affects regular attendees.

3. A misspelled lab name quietly becomes its own lab

What you'll notice: "Bio-Photonics Lab" and "BioPhotonics Lab" show up as two separate labs — with their own separate discount row in Group Discounts and their own separate line in Reports — even though everyone meant the same lab.

Why it happens: Lab/Group is a free-text field on each person, not a fixed list you choose from — so any two different spellings are, as far as the app is concerned, two different labs.

Workaround: agree on one spelling for each lab, and if you spot a typo, edit that person's record and correct the spelling to match everyone else. See Labs & departments.

4. There's no undo button, anywhere

What you'll notice: once you confirm an action — delete, clear, retire — there's no "undo" to bring it back. Confirmation dialogs are the only safety net before the action happens.

Why it happens: the app doesn't keep a history of past states to roll back to; it simply is whatever's currently in the database.

Workaround: make backups a habit, not an afterthought. Export a backup before any big change, and turn on Automatic Daily Backup (and, if you're on Chrome or Edge, the silent backup folder) so you're always a few clicks from a recent copy.

5. Restoring a backup replaces everything, with no merge

What you'll notice: restoring an older backup file wipes out anything you've entered since — there's no option to keep both sets of data.

Why it happens: a restore fully swaps the database out from under the app; there's no reliable, automatic way to merge two independent sets of projects and bookings.

Workaround: export a fresh backup of your current data before restoring anything else — see Restoring a backup.

6. The silent backup folder can lose permission after a restart

What you'll notice: silent backups stop showing up in the folder you chose, with no error to explain why.

Why it happens: Chrome and Edge can forget the permission you granted to write into that folder once the browser itself restarts — this is a browser-level privacy protection, not something the tracker controls.

Workaround: open Settings and click Re-enable under the Silent Backup Folder option to grant access again. See The silent backup folder.

7. The "Single Instrument Booking?" question asks every single time

What you'll notice: the first time you pick an instrument on a new booking, a prompt asks whether this is a single-instrument booking — and it asks again on the very next new booking, with no "don't ask again" option.

Why it happens: the app treats this as a per-booking question rather than a remembered preference, so it always starts fresh.

Workaround: just answer it each time — if you're not sure, choose No; you can always remove an instrument badge afterward if you change your mind.

8. "Emailing" attendees is really copy, paste, and send yourself

What you'll notice: the Email Attendees feature opens a blank email in your own mail app instead of sending anything itself, and you have to paste the recipients, subject, and body in yourself.

Why it happens: this is deliberate, not a bug — the tracker has no server of its own that could send mail on your behalf, and mail-app links have a length limit too short to reliably carry a pre-filled message. See Why it can't just send the email.

Workaround: use the three copy buttons in the dialog, then paste each one — recipients, subject, body — into the blank email the app opened for you.

9. A brand-new lab or department name only sticks if you actually save

What you'll notice: you type a new Lab/Group or Department name using "+ Add New" while editing a person, then close the form without saving — and the new name is nowhere to be found next time.

Why it happens: a new lab or department name isn't stored anywhere on its own; it only becomes real once it's attached to a person record that's actually been saved.

Workaround: if you're adding a new lab or department name, make sure you finish saving that person's record in the same sitting — don't cancel partway through.

10. You can't force-delete a person or instrument that has history

What you'll notice: the same button that deletes a person or instrument with no history instead retires one that has any — you never get a choice to override that and delete anyway.

Why it happens: this is deliberate — deleting a record with history would silently break every past booking, project, or milestone that refers to it. See Retire vs. delete.

Workaround: if you're trying to merge two duplicate records (say, the same person entered twice), retire the duplicate rather than trying to delete it — the retired one stays visible on its old records but drops out of pickers for new work.

Other small things worth knowing

  • Milestone status only cycles, it doesn't let you pick directly: clicking a milestone's status moves it to the next one in order (pending → in-progress → done → back to pending) — there's no dropdown to jump straight to a status. See Milestones.
  • Reports has no charts, and its export is spreadsheet-only: every card on the Reports screen is a table with numbers, and the export button always produces an XLSX spreadsheet — there's no chart view and no DOCX/PDF option there. See Reports & Utilization.
  • The calendar only shows one month at a time, and "Today" opens a dialog rather than scrolling: there's no week view, and clicking the Today button opens the Today's Agenda summary instead of jumping the grid to today's date. See Today's Agenda.
  • Booking conflicts are only caught when you save: the app doesn't warn you about a double-booked instrument or staff member while you're still filling out the form — only when you click save does it check and, if there's a clash, refuse with an error. See Conflicts.
